## Inspect and reproduce

Extract each downloaded ZIP into its own directory; both archives contain files directly at their root. For the mapping, read `README.md`, `CAPABILITIES.json` and `INPUT_BINDINGS.json`, then use Python 3.12 with NumPy:

```text
python -B reproduce.py --out ../math-capability-replay
```

That is the producer's reproduction command. The intake verified integrity; it did not independently replay or scientifically admit the new mapping.

For the project update, run `python -B verify.py` after extraction. Its `releases/` directory contains the immutable companion and Recovery packages, each with its own manifest and reproduction instructions. `CONTRIBUTIONS.json` distinguishes their status. `RECOVERY_NEXT_EXPERIMENT.md` asks a domain owner to supply a calibrated physical observation and pulse model; no experiment has been run or scheduled by this intake.

Recovery's examples concern a positive stationary relaxation mixture, declared pulse response and deterministic uncertainty enclosures. Synthetic agreement does not validate a physical model. A failed prediction rejects the joint premises; it does not identify a unique biological or quantum mechanism. The supplied E01 and Quantum Discrimination review notes establish only their stated bounded scopes.
