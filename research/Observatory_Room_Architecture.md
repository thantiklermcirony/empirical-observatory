# An observatory that can change its organisation

12 September 2026. Engineering contract; this is not a new scientific result.

The central organising question is: **which different states remain compatible with the observation, and which affordable measurement would separate them?** The programme's boundedness, temporal grammar, adaptation, resource models, quantum models and empirical records can cooperate on this question without asserting that their mathematics is interchangeable.

## Spatial catalogue and permanent instruments

`lib/observatory-catalogue.ts` is the source of truth for room entrances, physical hotspots, quick controls, instrument identities, source links, related instruments and room aliases. `/catalogue` makes the organisation inspectable; `/api/catalogue` supplies the same records to agents. The office dossier drawer and the experiment floor use this catalogue.

Eleven broad rooms organise mathematics, physics, biology, engineering, psychology, chemistry, Earth/environment, astronomy, computing, social science and the shared research archive. Subfields are indexed within them. This is an extensible navigation taxonomy, not an exhaustive ontology of science or a claim that every listed discipline has a working experiment. Chemistry and Astronomy have construction rooms; related admitted work remains accessible through cross-links.

Every device has an immutable `id` and `/devices/{id}` address. A device's home is determined by its single placement in a room. Its `source`, actual execution target, result kind and cross-references remain independent of that placement. Moving it means transferring one placement entry. It does not change an equation, a stored record or an experiment identity.

To collapse a room, set its `mergedInto` to an existing room ID. Old room links resolve to the destination. Inherited instruments appear in quick access, without falsely attaching their old coordinates to a different photograph. Place them deliberately in the new room before deleting the old physical placements. To expand, add a new room with a distinct scene and transfer placements; preserve the old device IDs. Old branch URLs such as `/labs/quantum`, `/labs/dynamics` and `/labs/temporal` remain aliases.

Catalogue checks reject duplicate identities, missing or multiple homes, invalid hotspots, broken related-device links and cyclic merges. Tests additionally verify referenced source files and scene assets. Public visitors inspect the catalogue; editing the public organisation remains a source-reviewed change. There is no unauthenticated administration endpoint.

## Rooms are the interface

A branch opens directly on its room. Numbered physical objects open its working instruments; a short quick-access row offers the same functions for keyboard and touch. The room selector shows the destination's actual scene before entry. Unavailable instruments open their construction note and useful cross-links rather than an invented result.

Question instruments share one reviewed inquiry engine, but use different housings: paper, clinical, oscilloscope, amber console, reel-recorder and teletype. A generic computer is not placed into every room. Photographs are imagined places, not photographs of operating scientific facilities; graph values come from calculations or declared source records.

Opened instruments remain mounted while their room is open, so closing and reopening the panel retains the current work. Same-origin visibility messages pause the root simulation/measurement runners and the synthetic family world while hidden. Explicit export or the central private-save action remains necessary before navigating away, refreshing or changing devices outside the room. IDA uses its own full-page workbench and native recording/save navigation guard; it is not destroyed as an iframe when a room dialog closes.

## What the central computer can actually execute

The spatial catalogue does not expand the router's scientific permissions. `/api/inquiry` declares reviewed computations. `/api/inquiry/research` plans, executes applicable contracts and synthesises their evidence when hosted AI is configured. Its shared allowance remains 200 provider requests per UTC day, including failed provider calls. A room address or source archive is not an arbitrary-code execution capability.

Current fixed calculations, public-data retrieval, exact-source printouts, optional private storage and encyclopedia references remain connected. Saved links to legacy branch pages retain their reports. The bounded rule reasoner and finite motion-learning workbench are now discoverable instruments; neither is an unrestricted inference engine. The reasoner exposes proof dependencies and missing premises, not a general contradiction checker.

## IDA integration and admission boundary

`/ida` opens the complete local browser application under `public/ida/workbench/`: seeded synthetic acquisition, raw CSV replay, optional Muse Bluetooth, fixed-reference geometry, five chart representations, two response protocols, video presentation, local IndexedDB sessions, export, WebXR and an experimental peer link. Numerical and worker modules are preserved from the reviewed package, with their tests carried into the Observatory test command.

The imported copy changes navigation, readable labels, source/credit links and the native navigation guard around starting and saving. It does not change the feature, distance, residue or cue equations. The reference is operator-declared rest with in-fit thresholds; it is not independently validated task competence. The synthetic signal responds by construction to demand. No diagnosis, mind decoding, induced emotion or validated recovery controller is claimed.

Raw EEG is not automatically sent to the central computer or an AI provider. Local export is deliberate. Optional peer links share feature summaries and task/timing messages. Hardware acquisition, headset compatibility and physiological validity remain separate acceptance gates.

The full original source/movie package is retained for publication. The browser movie is a distinct 768×432, 24 fps H.264 delivery rendition, with the complete 433-second edit and copied audio. Its own media hash and cue manifest are recorded; original and delivery stimuli must not be pooled as identical without testing delivery conditions. Credits and CC BY-SA notices accompany it. The two procedural pilot videos remain intact.

## Next scientific experiment

Keep `research/Next_Observatory_Experiment.md` as the prospective baseline contract. Its first target is calibrated observation sufficiency: identical reduced fraction, different compatible resource ceilings, and the additional measurement that resolves the ambiguity. The exact witness excludes a 0.600 mM target at a 0.565 mM necessary ceiling but does not establish attainability at a 0.625 mM ceiling.

The conventional calibrated resource ledger is the comparison to beat at equal measurement cost. Held-out cases, false exclusions, coverage and interval width are specified before execution. The proposed geometry/measurement selector must meet every frozen success criterion; an informative null result is acceptable. This protocol remains proposed. A redesigned website, successful software tests and publication do not execute or validate it.

## Direction beyond this release

The next programme-wide abstraction should be an investigation record with: observation identity and clock; compatible states; explicit candidate mechanisms; source and calibration lineage; competing predictions; missing inputs already sought; the next discriminating test and its cost; execution evidence; and a revision that marks affected conclusions for reassessment.

Admit a new lab computation only after its input units, preparation, applicable domain, baseline, negative controls, output kind, failure modes, provenance and tests are registered. A changed assumption should invalidate the affected inference, not erase unrelated evidence. Automatic scientific reorganisation or discovery is not implemented by the room catalogue. Its present adaptation is structural, inspectable and reversible.
