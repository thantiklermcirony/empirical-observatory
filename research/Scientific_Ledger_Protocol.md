# The shared scientific ledger — admission protocol 1

The Observatory indexes evidence; it does not make empirical premises true by declaring them. The shared runtime owns executable contracts. Rooms are movable views and retain stable instrument identities.

## Canonical sources

- `lib/science-fields.ts`: 42 adapted OECD Revised FOS 2007 field homes. The classification is a coverage scaffold, not complete science.
- `lib/scientific-vocabulary.ts`: scoped seed concepts, aliases and illustrative unit compatibility. These authored definitions are not imported ontology equivalences.
- `lib/engine/temporal-router.ts`: four canonical capability contracts, strict inquiry compilation and execution.
- `lib/ledger-runtime.ts`: one trusted implementation configuration consumed by the central inquiry and ledger demonstration.
- `lib/scientific-ledger.ts`: federated map of those records and existing room/device/source inventories.
- `lib/science-registry.ts`, `lib/research-cases.ts`, `lib/discovery-ledger.ts`: preserved claim, case and contribution records.

The legacy instruments remain source-indexed. Until each acquires a complete shared contract, they must not be advertised as fully governed by this ledger. Catalogue availability is different from registered execution.

## Admission record

1. Identify a concept using stable ID, preferred scientific label, aliases, a scoped definition, reference context and conflicting meanings. Scientific use does not establish that an entity exists. Hypothesised entities stay hypotheses.
2. Identify the measurand and observation procedure separately. Record system, preparation, clock, units, acquisition, uncertainty, calibration and source identity. Same dimension is not same quantity; same word is not same concept.
3. Register each operation with typed inputs and outputs, required premises, jurisdiction, clock, implementation identity, result class and known failure cases. A model-specific predicate cannot silently become a universal physical law.
4. Identify a claim with evidence class, exact statement, dependencies, supporting and contrary evidence, comparator, scope and a prospective falsifying observation. Computational success alone cannot admit an empirical claim.
5. Review a candidate through the existing editor workflow. Source revisions preserve the prior record. New vocabulary, adapters and claims need tests and review before publication. A proposal and a structural validation result are not an admission credential.
6. When a premise or observation changes, compute transitive dependants and mark them for reassessment. A corrected observation at one time is different from a later observation. Do not overwrite old receipts.

## First migration experiment: measured evidence

Use preserved BIO-PD-001 revision 2 as the baseline. Freeze its source hashes, inclusion counts, exclusions, replay outputs and uncertainty limitations. Map the observation identity and every calculation dependency into shared contracts without refitting or retuning the inspected dataset.

Success: numerical and inclusion parity with the frozen replay; changed source, units, preparation or observation identity invalidates the affected result; unsupported clinical or causal transfer is rejected; related rooms read one case ID and revision. Failure: any changed result without a declared revision, any omitted dependency, or any invalid transfer admitted. This is an engineering regression experiment, not new biological validation. Independent biological evidence remains a separate gate.

## Future formal verification

Lean may certify a precisely stated implication under its admitted axioms. Admission requires a pinned toolchain, checked declarations, axiom inspection and review of the mapping from observations to formal premises. No Lean certificate is claimed in this version.
