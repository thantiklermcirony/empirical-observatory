# Programme status — 12 September 2026

The Observatory is being developed as an executable observer framework: describe a system and its environment, state the permitted operations and constraints, run an appropriate model, and inspect what follows when an assumption or measurement changes. The programme connects UHL/composition, time and dynamics, adaptive resources, observation and evidence. No single experiment or state-sufficiency test represents its full scope.

## Published in this update

The quantum experiment now validates its six-state probability distribution before both planning and posterior updating. Empty, sparse, negative, nonfinite and incorrectly normalized priors are rejected. Valid point priors retain zero reducible uncertainty. The original normalization tolerance and valid-input behaviour are preserved.

The public-source candidate passed **57 Node tests and TypeScript checking**, including two new quantum contract tests. This is a software-correctness repair, not a new physical result. The change is in `lib/engine/quantum.ts`; its regression tests are in `tests/quantum-contract.test.ts` and are included in the ordinary test command.

## The next connected release

Three specialist workstreams are undergoing independent review. They are **local development work, not yet shipped as laboratories in this repository or the hosted application**.

| Workstream | What the reviewed work establishes | Next delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematical atlas | Executed conditional composition, resource and connection examples; source-specific counterexamples and explicit proof boundaries | A coherent source-bound package, trusted premise admission and a resource/diagnostic-deadline example |
| Quantum mechanisms | A four-level reference model retaining coherence, environmental dephasing and both reaction products; nine supplied test methods and 24 additional analytic checks passed in an independent snapshot review | Robust input admission, a reproducible published-curve importer and a frozen experiment package |
| Cellular adaptation | A finite-cofactor peroxide model whose ordinary kinetic comparator agrees; 24 validation checks and reciprocal synthetic benchmarks reproduced | Working scenario inputs, precise protocol provenance and a complete measurement/recovery comparison |

The biology parameter grid contains 27 unweighted constructed scenarios: 11 meet both illustrative recovery margins, seven meet only the signal margin, three meet only the repeat-challenge margin, and six meet neither. These are model scenarios, not population frequencies. The useful question is which measurement and intervention can distinguish remaining capacity under the declared mechanism; a recovered signal alone is not a calibrated measure of biological recovery.

The quantum reference is not a cryptochrome model or an established quantum-biological effect. Reproducing optical summary curves does not supply missing replicate records, a reaction-yield calibration or a cellular flux. The mathematical examples establish conclusions under their premises; fixed examples are not a general-purpose automated proof system.

## Integration and review gates

The common inquiry record carries the question, system, observer, quantities, operations, constraints, mechanism, premises, execution, results, contrast, next question and provenance under a versioned identity. Specialist solvers retain their own mathematical and measurement meanings.

The next integration must demonstrate a baseline, a changed operation, a removed necessary premise and a source change. Affected conclusions must recompute, narrow or become unavailable; unrelated conclusions should remain accessible. The same record should drive the explanation and visual display.

Four development agents now share a local coordination board. Round-trip communication and isolated project reviews have been exercised, and specific corrections have been assigned. The private board, local task identifiers and working directories are not part of this public repository.
