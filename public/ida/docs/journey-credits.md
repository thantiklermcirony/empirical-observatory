# Real-world journey: sources and edit record

Duration: 7:13. Real footage; an editorial montage, not one event and not a chronological history. No claim of maximum or universal emotional response.

Adapted audiovisual compilation: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Public-domain source portions remain public domain. Share this credit file with the movie. Contributors and depicted people do not endorse this experiment.

Edits: excerpts, resizing/pillarboxing, audio normalization, one-second cross-dissolves. The final beach repeats the opening segment. No fake crying, screams, replacement speech or AI-generated scene footage.

The King speech audio is redacted in the archive edition. This cut preserves that silence. It does not contain an audible I Have a Dream speech. Ground Zero footage is the September 14 address, not live footage of the attacks. Hindenburg is a historical airship disaster, not a road accident. This version does not include construction footage or a montage of bereaved people crying.

## Measurement use

Use a fixed virtual cinema screen. Collect reference and consent/content preview before playback; collect ratings afterwards. The movie has no reading tasks, deliberate blank recovery screens or in-film rating requests. Source signs and captions visible in original footage may remain.

Do not assign an emotion from scene identity. The beach is an exposure, not guaranteed rest. History, familiarity, political views, hearing, movement and source sound all affect interpretation. Crossfades and carryover are part of the stimulus; exclude transition windows for scene summaries and retain them when studying transitions. Compare whole-window EEG change and subjective reports across independent sessions. No automatic emotion decoder or safety validation has been established.

This download/edit was selected by accessible provenance and relevance, not ranked popularity. The original claim about using the most popular news coverage is not substantiated.
