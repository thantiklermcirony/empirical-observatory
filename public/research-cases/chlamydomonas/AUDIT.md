# Evidence corrections made before this build

Earlier artifacts are retained, but the following claims must not be inherited
as evidence for this release.

1. `whole_system_lab/anomaly_shake.py` specifies the allegedly diverging and
   repaired outcomes directly. Those examples are illustrative witnesses, not
   seven independently simulated biological discoveries. The source does not
   establish general missing-sensor recovery or wet-lab validation.
2. `whole_system_lab/connector_mesh.py` calls improvement over the better
   singleton predictor “synergy.” That is not a general information-theoretic
   synergy measure. Reducing the number of encoded labels is not reducing the
   measurement cost or creating information. Its coordinates are not physical
   higher-dimensional geometry.
3. The binary E. coli demonstration encodes stipulated bit transitions. Its
   ATP, PMF, movement and growth bits are not calibrated physical balances or a
   real organism's lifecycle. The new simulator does not treat them as data.

New calculations in this release use imported published ODEs and original
measurements. The numerical and empirical tests have real failure routes:

- 34 of 1,000 division counts change when the initial grid is refined.
- A 72.7-minute candidate hidden-state effect disappears with the independent
  solver; it is explicitly rejected as a numerical artifact.
- Adding growth history to the chosen observational predictor worsens the
  overall held-out score. The negative result is retained.

The surviving light-timing contrast is a model prediction from established
mechanisms. It is not labelled an independently observed biological discovery.
