Where the equations actually connect

A connector must preserve quantity, units, time and meaning. Shared words alone do not authorize a link. This is an equation-dependency diagram, not a physical arrangement of molecules.

The exact bookkeeping connection

Total TF = free TF + complex. Total inhibitor = free inhibitor + complex + phosphorylated inhibitor. Internal reactions cancel in these totals, leaving simple synthesis/decay laws. Division halves every amount and the volume, preserving concentrations and the summed daughter amounts.

The implemented analytical reduction uses those identities. It is algebra applied to an existing model, not a newly discovered biological law. A pixel-area measurement cannot be plugged into model volume without a calibrated transformation.

What this does and does not establish

These are observational predictions, not causal experiments. Area is measured in image pixels; it is not silently converted into the simulation's arbitrary volume. Endpoint size and future measurements never enter the predictors. Four experiments from one study are not four independent laboratories.

The source equations were not fitted here to these cells. This is a test of an observation panel, not validation of a complete organism model.

The unfinished organism, made explicit

No hidden substitute dials. These modules need measured mechanisms and joint validation before the Observatory can use them to predict a cell's life.

Unsupported

Nutrients & water

Need nutrient uptake, carbon/nitrogen reserves, osmosis and growth data under joint perturbations. Changing μ is not a nutrient-dose simulation.

Unsupported

Electricity & energy

Need ion flux, membrane potential, photosynthetic electron transport and ATP balance. Binary light is a control signal, not a photon or energy ledger.

Unsupported

Motion & morphology

Need calibrated 3D shape, flagellar mechanics, fluid drag and phototaxis. The cell drawing does not provide those equations.

Unsupported

Death, sex & evolution

Need damage/repair, viability and lifespan, mating and zygote data, stochastic inheritance and selection. This model covers repeated asexual divisions only.
