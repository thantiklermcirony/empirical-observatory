# The Observatory as an operating research programme

The destination is an open instrument for learning which observations and histories improve a specified prediction or decision. Its central method is shared; biological, physical, computational and philosophical claims remain individually testable. The programme's papers supply questions and candidate descriptions, not automatic acceptance of an implementation.

## One loop, five responsibilities

1. **Observe:** adapters preserve identities, units, actual observation times, first receipt, missingness and source terms.
2. **Describe:** a versioned candidate states what it remembers and what it discards. Compare it with appropriate existing methods.
3. **Predict:** record the allowed inputs, target, model and forecast before scoring. Retrospective replay is labelled separately from prospective collection.
4. **Challenge:** independently check chronology, scoring, failure accounting and conventional comparisons. A failed observation prerequisite blocks promotion even when a later numerical score looks favourable.
5. **Revise:** generate a traceable proposal; test it on fresh evidence; require recorded review before admission. Previous contracts and failures stay available.

## How the scientific programme connects

| Research line | Role in the instrument | Evidence needed for advancement |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations / UHL / geometry | Supply explicit questions about adequate state, transformations and observer-dependent description | A mathematical result under specified assumptions or a separate discriminating empirical test |
| Recovery / redox / longevity | Test whether measured recovery history adds useful predictive information | Longitudinal observation quality, strong history and duration models, independent held-out organisms |
| IDA / cognition | Choose measurements that distinguish competing state descriptions | Prospective comparisons of measurement cost and decision quality; hardware observations remain separate from simulation |
| TAO / action | Test intervention order, recovery and controlled behaviour | Matched-information controller comparisons and measured outcomes |
| Earth / space | Resolve forecasts against outcomes arriving later | Exact lead times, preserved vintages, settled outcomes, missing-slot accounting and strong physical competitors |
| AI / software contributions | Make memory, timing and evidence contracts reliable in useful systems | Reproduced defects, maintained regression checks, upstream review, and independent AI benchmarks where claimed |

## This release's bounded automation

The shared ledger records frozen contracts, snapshots, predictions, outcomes, checks and candidate review states. Its local hashes detect changed records; local timestamps alone are not public proof of prior issuance. GitHub commits and run records provide an additional publication trail.

The GB collector saves official national forecasts 24–24.5 hours before a half-hour target interval, preserving the exact actual lead. It waits at least 48 hours after that interval ends before settling the provider's estimated actual. Later revisions are distinct. This phase collects and verifies the incumbent; it does not fit or advertise an improved predictor.

A deterministic planner reads the evidence and records the next useful work. Missing follow-up leads to a proposal for better measurements; failed scores do not trigger undeclared feature searches. GitHub checks exercise adapters and contracts. A scheduled collector updates evidence, and a quiet supervisor checks for failures or work requiring review.

Model creation, mathematical discovery, shared conclusions and model promotion do not occur autonomously in this release. The system should only be called self-improving after successive admitted changes improve fresh predictive or decision outcomes under an unchanged comparison process, including unsuccessful proposals and their costs.

## Recovery Lab's first finding

The source contains 289 aged mice and 6,317 assessments. A faithful Python adapter reproduces the original authors' 270-animal, 5,957-visit selected analysis while retaining all original exits separately. Two animals have contradictory exit chronology and are quarantined for endpoint analysis. Missing measurements are bounded rather than coded healthy.

The initial availability inspection resolved 3,451 of 4,104 eligible DO landmarks (84.09%). A stricter survival-evidence review removed six unsupported labels, leaving 3,445/4,104 (83.94%), below the fixed 90% prerequisite. Coherent two-week windows also failed. All six availability-only window checks preceded fitting and remain recorded. The original 5–9-day window was retained as the descriptive endpoint. Its completed added-history candidate improved primary error by 0.84%, but worsened error in both fixed sensitivity analyses; every numeric and overall gate failed. A favourable score cannot override the failed prerequisite.

This is a direct input to the research queue: obtain more complete follow-up, preserve why observations are missing, and design a fresh comparison. Descriptive model results can identify implementation and measurement questions; they do not establish improved healthy lifespan or treatment benefit.

## Delivery sequence

**Now:** complete the audited Recovery Lab replay, shared ledger, live collection and explicit review queue. Publish source, tests, original contracts, missingness and failed gates alongside the interface.

**After enough prospective grid outcomes:** qualify receipt/settlement reliability; specify the smallest useful improvement and fixed model family; freeze the candidate before a future comparison period. No retrospective vintage reconstruction.

**Next biological experiment:** identify a more complete longitudinal recovery dataset or a laboratory collaboration. Redox exposure schedules can test mechanism and intervention order. CITP can separately test the transfer of survival effects across experimental contexts.

**Then:** expose stable forecast/evidence exports to God's Eye View or another maintained visualization. The interface displays timestamped predictions, uncertainty and later outcomes, without equating visual connections with established causal structure.

Each step produces an independently usable artifact: an adapter, a benchmark, a falsifiable result, a prediction record, or a tested software contribution. Broader claims require successful independent tests across the relevant domains.
